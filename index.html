Questions

The things people ask first

Who owns the website afterwards?

You do — the domain, the hosting account and the site itself are all in your name. If you ever want to move to someone else, nothing is locked to me.

Do I need to be good with computers?

No. Editing your text and photos works a bit like filling in a form, and I'll walk you through it once it's live. If you'd rather I made the changes, the care plan covers that.

How long does it take?

It depends on how quickly I get your details and photos, which is usually the slow part rather than the building. I'll give you a realistic date on our first call rather than a sales promise here.

Is there a monthly fee?

Yes — £20 a month on a Forge site, on top of the one-off build cost. That covers hosting, keeping things updated, and small fixes when you need them. Foundry is £49 a month because the automation needs looking after too. Nothing is on a long contract.

Can you just do the automation, without a website?

Yes. If your site is fine and it's the admin that's the problem, ring and describe what you're repeating every week and I'll tell you whether it's worth automating.

What if I don't like it?

That's what the two revision rounds are for, and why you see progress as it's built rather than a reveal at the end. If it isn't right, we keep working until it is.
