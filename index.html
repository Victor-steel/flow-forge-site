Common questions

Who owns the website afterwards?

On Forge, yes — the domain, hosting and site stay in your name. Foundry is a managed service, so if you cancel, the website, domain, hosting and automations end with the subscription.

Do I need to be technical?

No. Updating text and photos is closer to filling in a form. I will walk you through it once the site is live, or handle changes for you on the care plan.

How long does a project take?

Timing usually depends on how quickly photos and details arrive. You will get a realistic date on the first call, not a vague promise here.

Is there a monthly fee?

Forge includes £20 a month after launch for hosting and light care. Foundry is £297 a month all-in. Neither locks you into a long contract; a month’s notice is enough.

Can you set up automation without a new website?

Yes. If your site is already solid and the admin is the problem, call and describe the tasks you keep repeating. I will tell you honestly whether automation is worth it.

What if the design is not right first time?

That is what revision rounds and live progress links are for. We keep refining until you are happy to go live.
