Straight answers

Who owns the website afterwards?

You do - domain, hosting and the site itself, on both packages. Stop Foundry and you keep the site; automation and support are what end.

Do I need to be good with computers?

No. Editing is closer to filling in a form. I’ll walk you through it once it’s live - or handle changes on the care plan.

How long does it take?

Mostly depends how fast photos and details arrive. You’ll get a realistic date on the first call, not a sales promise here.

Is there a monthly fee?

Forge is £20 a month after the build. Foundry is £297 a month all-in. Neither is a long contract - a month’s notice and you’re done.

Can you just do the automation?

Yes. If the site is fine and the admin isn’t, call and describe what you’re repeating every week.

What if I don’t like it?

That’s what revision rounds and progress links are for. We keep going until it’s right.
