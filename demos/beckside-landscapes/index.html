The gardening year, and where we are in it

Landscaping in Cumbria is not a twelve-month free-for-all. Some jobs only work in certain months, and the good slots go early. This is what we are usually doing, and when it is worth ringing.

Three things, done properly

Most gardens need some of all three. We would rather do the drawing, the build and the upkeep than hand you over halfway.

Design and drawing

A scaled plan and a planting list before anyone digs, so you are approving a garden rather than hoping.

  • Site survey and levels
  • Scaled layout plan, 1:100 or 1:50
  • Planting plan with a full species list
  • Materials samples, on site, in the rain
  • Costed in stages if it needs splitting

Hard landscaping

The heavy end. Stone, levels and drainage — the parts that decide whether a garden lasts twenty years or five.

  • Slate and sandstone terraces
  • Dry stone walling, new and rebuilt
  • Steps, paths and setted drives
  • Sleeper and gabion retaining
  • Land drains, soakaways and gullies
  • Fencing, gates and timber decks

Grounds and garden care

Regular visits on a fixed monthly figure, or a one-off rescue for a garden that has got away from someone.

  • Fortnightly or monthly maintenance
  • Hedge cutting and reductions
  • Borders: pruning, splitting, mulching
  • Holiday-let changeover tidies
  • Leaf clearing and gutter checks
  • Storm callouts for regulars

How a job runs

Four stages, in this order, every time. You will know which one you are in.

  1. 01

    Site visit

    We come out, walk it with you and take levels, photos and a soil check. Free inside 30 miles of Kendal, and we will tell you there and then if it is not a job for us.

  2. 02

    Drawing

    A scaled plan and a planting list, on paper, so you can move a path before it costs anything to move. Two rounds of changes included.

  3. 03

    Fixed quote

    Priced line by line, with labour, materials and muck-away separated so you can see what moves if the budget has to. It holds for 90 days.

  4. 04

    Build

    One crew from first dig to last sweep, no subbing out. Photos at the end of each week if you are not local, and a two-year guarantee on everything we lay.
