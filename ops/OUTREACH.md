## Personalisation hooks (pick one)
- Saw you’re mainly on Instagram — hard for new clients to find hours / book
- Google shows no proper site / outdated page
- Competitors nearby have a simple site and you’re stronger on reviews/photos
- New season / bridal / colour work deserves a clean page to send people to

## Do / don’t
- Do: short emails, local angle, one ask
- Don’t: blast 50 identical emails, invent case studies, hard-sell Foundry on first touch

## Call script (if you ring them)
Keep it under 60 seconds. Smile in your voice. If they’re with a client, offer to call back.

**Opener**
> Hi, is this [Salon name]? … Brilliant — my name’s Luke from Flow Forge in Carlisle. Have you got 30 seconds?

**Pitch**
> I help local salons with a simple website — nothing fancy. A lot of places are strong on Instagram but when new clients Google them, hours and booking are hard to find. I put up a clean site for £497 — a few pages, contact form, basic SEO, and a small chatbot. Half to start, half when it’s live.

**Ask**
> Would it be useful if I took a quick look at how you show up online and just told you honestly whether it’s worth it?

**If yes**
> Great — what’s the best email, and is there a quiet time later this week for a 10-minute call? I’ll send a couple of examples: withflowforge.com.

**If busy / not now**
> No worries — I can email you a one-pager instead. What’s the best address?

**If no**
> Totally fine — thanks for your time. Have a good day.

**Objections (short)**
- *“We already have Instagram.”* → Perfect for regulars; Google searchers still need hours/book. The site points them to you.
- *“Too expensive.”* → It’s a one-off £497, not a monthly agency fee. Happy to show what’s included.
- *“Send me something.”* → Will do — email + withflowforge.com. Follow up in 3 days if no reply.
